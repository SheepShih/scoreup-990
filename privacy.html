Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Score Up 990 ("the app") is developed by Sheep Shih.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

  • Your study records, settings, stickers and goal score are saved only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to any server run by the developer.

Notifications

If you turn on weekday reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No information is sent anywhere.

Speech

Listening audio and pronunciation are generated on your device by iOS. Your voice is never recorded.

Deleting your data

You can delete all study records in Settings > Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
