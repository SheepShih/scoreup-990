Frequently asked questions

When can I study?

Sessions are available on weekdays (Monday to Friday). Weekends are rest days. You can still check your Diary every day.

How is my score estimated?

Every question has a level. Correct answers on harder questions raise your estimate more. Listening and Reading are scored separately, and your total is the average of the two. The estimate appears after at least 5 Listening and 5 Reading questions. See Diary > ? (How scores work) for details.

Is this an official test score?

No. Scores are estimates based on your answers in this app. TOEIC is a registered trademark of ETS. This app is not endorsed or approved by ETS.

Can I change the session length or accent?

Yes. Tap the minutes on the Study tab to choose 30 or 60 minutes. Change the accent and speech speed in Settings.

How do I start over?

Settings > Reset all progress. This deletes all study records on your device.
